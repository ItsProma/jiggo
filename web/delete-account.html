Jiggo account and data deletion

Jiggo by Deb Creatives · Android and Web

You can request deletion of your Jiggo account and its associated data from inside the app or by contacting support from this page. You do not need to reinstall the app to send an email request.

Delete selected data without deleting your account

You can delete individual saved documents and scan-history entries while keeping your Jiggo account.

Delete in the app

  1. Open Jiggo while signed into the account containing the data.
  2. Open Library and locate the document or scan-history entry.
  3. Tap its Delete (trash) button and confirm. Repeat for each item you want removed.

A successful deletion removes the selected Firestore record, including its saved title, document content or scan answer and associated item metadata. Other Library items and your account remain available. Export anything you want to keep before confirming.

Request selected-data deletion without reinstalling the app

Email jiggo800@gmail.com with the subject Delete selected Jiggo data. State whether you want specific documents, specific scans, all documents or all scan history removed, and explicitly say keep my Jiggo account. Identify items by title and approximate date; do not include private document contents.

If you used Google sign-in, send from the same Google email where possible. If you used guest sign-in, say whether you still have access. Support must verify account ownership before deleting cloud data. Without access or a reliable account identifier, a guest account may not be identifiable. Do not send passwords, login codes, identity documents or API keys.

Request selected-data deletion by email

This button opens your email app; review and send the message there. If it does not open, email the address above manually. Opening this page does not send a request or delete anything. Support will explain any verification steps and expected completion time in its reply.

What remains and retention

Deleting Library items does not delete your sign-in account, display name, age/permission declaration, submitted content reports, usage/rate-limit records or other saved items. These remaining records follow the retention practices in our Privacy Policy; Jiggo does not promise automatic deletion within 90 days or another fixed period. There is no automatic expiration configured for all operational records. You can request deletion of your account and associated data using the separate account-deletion steps below.

The selected item is removed from the active Firestore Library after the in-app deletion succeeds, or after support verifies and completes your request. Provider backups, security logs, previously processed AI data and support correspondence have separate retention practices. Files already exported to your device or shared to another service are not deleted by removing a Library item.

Delete your entire account

Delete inside Jiggo

  1. Open Jiggo while signed into the account you want to delete.
  2. Open Profile → Delete Account.
  3. Review the confirmation and confirm deletion. If prompted, sign in with the same Google account again.

Guest users can use the same Delete Account option while they still have access to their guest account. Deletion is irreversible; export anything you want to keep first. Your Google account itself is not deleted.

Request deletion by email

Email jiggo800@gmail.com with the subject Delete my Jiggo account. For a Google-linked account, send from the Google email address you used in Jiggo when possible. If you cannot access it, explain that so support can discuss a way to verify ownership.

For a guest account, say you used guest sign-in and whether you still have access. Without access or a reliable account identifier, support may not be able to identify or verify a guest account. Do not send passwords, login codes, identity documents or private document contents.

This form only prepares a message in your email app. Review it and press Send there to submit your request. If no email app opens, email the address above manually. This website does not automatically send an email or delete an account.

What happens next?

Support will review your email, verify ownership as needed and explain the next steps or any information required to complete deletion. Account data is not deleted merely by opening this page or preparing an email. If deletion fails after starting, some data may already be removed; contact support or retry the in-app deletion flow.

Data deleted

  • Your Jiggo Firebase Authentication account.
  • Saved documents, titles, edited content and scan history.
  • Your submitted AI content reports and age/permission declaration.
  • Account-specific AI quota, attempt and rate-limit records managed by Jiggo.

Data that can remain

A limited deletion-status record containing the former Firebase user ID, status, timestamps and job information is retained with no automatic expiration to prevent old requests recreating deleted content. App-wide request/completion and aggregate usage records without your Firebase user ID or submitted content can also remain. Provider security logs, backups and previously processed AI requests follow the providers' separate retention practices. Files you exported or shared are outside the Jiggo account and are not removed from your device or other services.

Support emails are separate correspondence. Ask in your request if you also want support-email deletion, subject to information needed to verify and record the deletion request. Read the Privacy Policy for more details.
