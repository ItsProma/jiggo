Privacy Policy

Jiggo by Deb Creatives · Last updated: 16 November 2026

This policy describes data handling in Jiggo, an AI question-solving and document-creation app for Android and Web. Contact the developer at jiggo800@gmail.com.

1. Age and permission

Jiggo is intended for users aged 13 or older. Do not use Jiggo if you are under 13. Before sign-in and before AI use, you are asked to declare an age group and acknowledge this policy. Users aged 13–17 are also asked to confirm a parent or guardian has reviewed Jiggo and permitted its use. These are declarations; Jiggo does not independently verify your identity, date of birth, or a guardian's identity through this flow. Additional consent requirements can apply where you live.

Jiggo stores the age band, permission declarations, policy version and declaration time, not your full date of birth. Contact support to correct a recorded age band. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal data, contact support so we can investigate and remove the relevant account data after verification.

2. Information used by Jiggo

  • Accounts: Firebase Authentication assigns a user ID. Google sign-in can provide your name, email address and profile image, along with sign-in information. Guest sign-in creates an anonymous account ID. Your edited display name is stored in Firebase Authentication.
  • Scan requests: the photo you choose, requested explanation language and related request data are sent to Jiggo's backend and OpenAI for AI processing and safety checks. Firestore scan history stores the generated answer, language, time and any title you set. The original scan image is not saved in Jiggo's Firestore history.
  • Documents: the title, type, language and instructions you provide are used to generate a document. Generated and edited document contents, titles and metadata are saved in your account Library.
  • Operational records: request counts, timestamps, request IDs, completion status and available token-usage metadata support quotas, cost control, troubleshooting and abuse prevention. Our application logging avoids logging submitted images, prompts, answers, API keys or arbitrary provider error bodies.
  • Reports and support: text and notes you deliberately submit through the AI content reporting feature are stored under your account for review. Emailing support shares your email address and whatever you put in the email with the developer and the email service.

Upload only the educational question or document needed for your request. Avoid faces, identification documents, passwords, contact details and sensitive information about yourself or other people. AI responses may be inaccurate; review them before relying on or sharing them.

3. Why information is processed

Information is used to sign you in, return requested AI results, maintain your Library, let you edit or export documents, manage eligibility and permission declarations, control costs and abuse, investigate content reports, answer support requests and complete account deletion.

PDFs are generated on your device or browser. If you choose to share or print a PDF, the app or service you select receives the file according to its own privacy practices.

4. Providers, hosting and AI processing

  • Google Firebase / Google Cloud: authentication, Firestore storage, Cloud Functions and App Check. App Check uses reCAPTCHA Enterprise on Web and Play Integrity in Android release builds. These services can process network, device and security signals such as IP addresses to operate and protect the app.
  • OpenAI: selected images, instructions and text needed for generation and moderation. Jiggo requests that Responses API application-state storage be disabled. This is not a promise of zero retention: OpenAI may retain data for abuse monitoring and other purposes described in its API data controls.
  • Vercel: hosts the Web app and these public pages. Visiting the website sends normal network requests, including IP address and browser request information, to the hosting provider.

Advertising: This version of Jiggo does not show advertisements or include the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Jiggo does not request advertising identifiers for advertising.

These providers may process information in countries other than your own. Provider terms and applicable data controls govern their independent processing. Relevant details: Google Privacy Policy, Firebase privacy information, OpenAI API data controls and Vercel Privacy Policy.

Optional notifications

Notification permission is optional. When enabled, Firebase Cloud Messaging registers this installation. Jiggo stores its messaging installation ID (FID), device registration ID, language, platform and last connection time under your account. Your permission choice is stored on this device too. Turning notifications off removes the messaging ID and retains a small revocation record to stop old requests re-enabling delivery. Account deletion removes account-specific notification and rate-limit records. No advertising SDK is added.

Handwriting notes

For handwriting recognition, the selected prepared photo is sent through Jiggo’s backend to OpenAI. Recognized text is saved as a Library document; corrections are saved when you choose Save changes. Original handwriting photos are not saved in Firestore history. You can copy, export a TXT file or share the text with an app you choose. Account or Library-item deletion removes saved notes under the existing deletion process.

Photo PDFs

Scan to PDF processes the photos you select on your device or browser, without an AI request. It may resize photos and removes camera EXIF metadata from the prepared copies. Draft pages are temporary until you save or download a PDF. Saving opens the Android document picker or a browser download. If you choose a cloud storage provider or share a PDF, that provider or app receives the file. These PDFs are not automatically uploaded to your Jiggo account Library.

Chat messages

Chat messages and AI replies are sent through Jiggo’s backend to OpenAI and saved under your account. Only a limited recent part of the conversation is supplied for each reply; Jiggo does not remember every older message. You can delete a chat from Chat history. Deletion removes its message text; small deletion and request metadata may remain to prevent old requests recreating it. Account deletion removes account-specific chats, request records and chat usage limits.

Optional voice input

When you start voice input, your device or browser speech service processes microphone audio and may send it to its provider online. Availability and language support depend on that service. Jiggo does not save audio files. Only text you accept is added to your document details; it is sent for AI processing when you choose to generate. You can type instead. Stop listening or close the voice dialog to end capture. Denying microphone permission does not prevent typed document creation.

5. Storage, access and security

Jiggo uses authenticated, owner-scoped access for your saved documents and scans, server-side AI credentials, App Check and request limits. Data is transmitted over HTTPS. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Your device or browser keeps sign-in state and may cache saved content. Logging out or uninstalling does not delete your cloud account. Clearing app or browser storage can remove local access to a guest account; use account deletion while you still have access if you want the cloud data removed.

6. Retention and deletion

Your saved documents and scans remain until you delete them or delete your account. Deleting one Library item removes that item, not your entire account or all security and usage records.

Successful account deletion removes the Firebase Authentication account, account Library, submitted reports, eligibility declaration and account-specific quota, attempt and rate-limit records managed by Jiggo. It does not delete your Google account or copies of files you exported or shared.

Limited operational records remain. A deletion-status marker containing the former Firebase user ID, deletion state, timestamps and job information prevents old or in-flight requests recreating data. This marker currently has no automatic expiration. App-wide request status and aggregate token/cost records do not store your Firebase user ID or submitted content, and can remain after account deletion. There is currently no guaranteed automatic deletion schedule for all operational records.

Provider security logs, backups, AI processing records and support emails have separate retention processes. An account deletion in Jiggo does not instantly erase every provider log or an exported file. Tell support if you also want help with a support email or a provider-related deletion request.

7. Your choices and account deletion

You can edit your display name, rename or delete Library items, export documents and delete your account from Profile → Delete Account. Google users may be asked to sign in again to confirm deletion. Guest users can delete while signed into the guest account.

You can also request deletion without reinstalling the app through the Jiggo account deletion page. Support may need to verify account ownership before acting. Depending on applicable law, you may have additional access, correction, deletion, objection or consent-withdrawal rights; contact the developer to make a request.

8. Changes and contact

Changes to data practices will be reflected here and in the app as appropriate. The update date identifies the latest revision. For questions, corrections, privacy requests or account-deletion help, email jiggo800@gmail.com.
